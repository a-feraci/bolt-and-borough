# Five Business Portfolio Challenge Overview

Working names for fictional portfolio businesses. Each release gets its own source, demo, test evidence and handoff. The first release of each project must prove its primary customer journey.

| Business and status | Customers, products and revenue | Business problem and customer journey |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bolt & Borough Mobile Auto** � Live MVP, initial testing complete | Baltimore-area drivers with limited time or transportation and local operators with small fleets. Scheduled diagnostics, oil changes, battery replacements and routine checks. Individual jobs, maintenance packages and fleet retainers. | Drivers need to know if service can come to them and what it might cost. Check ZIP  choose service  enter vehicle and preferred window  review a simulated request. Fleet managers go directly to a separate inquiry. |
| **Velvet Circuit Supply** � Planned | Keyboard hobbyists, gamers, designers and students. Artisan keycaps, desk mats, cable kits and coordinated workstation bundles. Product sales, bundles and seasonal drops. | Customers need aesthetic discovery and clear compatibility before choosing gear. Browse collection  filter options  inspect specs and availability  add to cart  simulate checkout. |
| **Northstar Desk** � Planned | Small organizations without full-time IT. Workstation setup, IT documentation, help desk, basic security reviews and staff training. Project fees, training and monthly retainers. | Owners need a plain-language way to understand support scope and recurring costs. Arrive through an industry or problem  compare plans  take a short needs assessment  preview a consultation request. |
| **Little Orbit Learning** � Planned | Curious adults and career switchers. Short lessons on digital literacy, practical technology and creative workflows. Free introductions plus premium subscriptions, standalone courses and organizational packages. | Time-constrained learners need to start quickly and know where to resume. Discover a course  sample a lesson  answer a quiz  see progress and resume next time. |
| **Afterglow Atlas** � Planned | Couples, friends, families and private event planners. Themed installations, pop-ups and small-group workshops. Tickets, workshops, venue buyouts and collaborations. | Visitors need enough atmosphere to choose an event and enough practical information to attend. Filter upcoming events  view details and accessibility  choose a demo ticket tier or open a private inquiry. |

## Release order

1. **Bolt & Borough** establishes the launch, QA and evidence workflow with one high-priority journey.
2. **Velvet Circuit** introduces a larger catalog and cart state.
3. **Northstar Desk** adds an explainable decision tool and B2B conversion flow.
4. **Little Orbit** adds durable browser-local progress and reusable lesson patterns.
5. **Afterglow Atlas** combines data-driven browsing and reservation state with heavier visual media.

For each site, capture a brief, research, wireframe, stack decision, MVP evidence and handoff. Move through Research  Planned  Building  Live MVP  Testing  Improving  Portfolio Updated. Record observed behavior before proposing improvements. Schedule no future launches until the preceding project's first release is reviewable.
